Brand overview & ethos

Who SPC University is, what it teaches, and the editorial posture that every asset in this kit is built to reinforce.

Identity

SPC University, also named the School of Private Civics. It teaches private ordering, equity, trusts, jurisdiction, administrative power, business structuring and asset protection.

Domain: spcuniversity.com. Member portal: member.spcuniversity.com.

Positioning

Doctrine taught in sequence. The site frames its work as a study, not a sermon: doctrine without order is mythology.

The stated mission is accurate, court-defensible knowledge — rejecting pseudolaw, mythology and fear-based marketing.

Audience

Self-directed learners: beginners starting at principle one, and advanced students working through case law, trust structures and private ordering.

Buyers are practical operators — owners, families, trustees, students of law.

Offerings

The SPC Doctrine Ebook and training system, case law breakdowns, foundational video lessons, trust and structuring concepts, live lectures, and a members' community with progress tracking.

Curricular spine

Eight principles in order: public vs private capacity, jurisdiction and classification, trust structures, equity and fiduciary relationships, administrative vs judicial power, business structuring, asset protection, and private ordering principles.

Primary taglines

Substance Over Form — the core editorial line.

Law • Commerce • Empowerment — the descriptor lockup.

Knowledge Is Power, Understanding Is Freedom — campaign line.

You have a right to privacy — site title line.

Editorial posture. The brand earns trust by being precise. Every asset in this kit avoids guaranteed outcomes, immunity claims and fear-based urgency. If a design needs a headline that promises a result, the headline is wrong, not the design.

Logo system & usage

Three marks: the primary chrome crest, the compact banner treatment, and the circular Atlas icon used for avatars and favicons.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the height of the letter S on every side of the crest. Never let type, borders or image edges enter that zone.

Minimum on-screen height: 120 px for the full crest, and 32 px for the compact banner. Below 120 px the fine lettering inside the crest stops resolving — use the compact version instead.

Do & don't

Do place the crest on deep navy or pure black, where the chrome and cyan read correctly.

Do use the Atlas icon alone for avatars, favicons and app tiles.

Don't recolour the crest, rotate it, add outlines, drop shadows or gradients behind it.

Don't stretch it, or place it on mid-tone or busy photography.

Resolution warning. The largest logo file available is about 1,600 px wide and there is no vector or transparent-background master. For print, signage or merchandise, request the original vector art from the brand owner. SPC University's terms state that its logos and branding are owned by SPC University.

Contrast rules

Body copy sits at WCAG AA or better: Chrome Silver on Midnight Navy is about 12.9:1, and the dimmed caption tone on the same navy is about 5.4:1.

Gold on navy is about 9.6:1 — safe for text. Never put cyan on gold, or gold on white: both fail. For gold buttons, use deep ink #201503 for the label, never white.

Ratio & proportion

Treat the palette as roughly 75% dark ground, 15% chrome and white, 7% cyan, 3% gold. Gold is a scalpel, not a paint bucket.

Cyan carries energy and interaction. Gold carries prestige and price. Mixing the two liberally turns a premium legal brand into a casino.

How these values were derived. There is no published SPC University brand guideline, so these hex values were measured from the live site's artwork, the ebook cover and the logo files. Treat them as an accurate reconstruction rather than official brand standards, and confirm with the brand owner before a print run.
04

Typography system

Three voices: a beveled, extended display face for authority; a warm serif for doctrine; a clean sans for reading. Exact font files are not published with the brand — these are the closest web-safe substitutes.

Display — headlines & cover titles

Substance Over Form

In the logo and on covers the display face is a bold, beveled, wide-set futuristic sans with metallic treatment. Web substitute: Orbitron (700–900) or Anton for condensed campaigns. Always uppercase, tight leading (0.95–1.05), slight negative tracking.

Editorial — subtitles & doctrine copy

Foundations Before Remedies

Ebook covers and doctrine headings use a warm serif, often italic for subtitles. Web substitute: Libre Baskerville or Georgia. Use italic sparingly, for framing lines only.

Reading — body, UI, captions

Distinguish the capacities you operate within and the consequences that follow each. How forums, status and classification determine the reach of any tribunal.

Web substitute: Inter or the system UI sans in this page. Body at 16–18 px, line-height 1.6–1.7, measure 60–75 characters.

Campaign accent. Social and YouTube work uses a distressed, heavy condensed yellow display face for punch lines. Reserve it for thumbnails and social only — never for body copy or the cover spine.

Imagery & visual language

Neoclassical authority lit like a product. Courthouse columns, Lady Justice, leather law volumes, a digital globe — rendered in high-contrast 3D against dark grounds.

Recurring motifs

Courthouse columns, a blindfolded Lady Justice with scales and sword, stacked leather books labelled LAW, TRUSTS, ASSETS, FINANCE, a digital globe, passports, a fountain pen, wax seals and an embossed eagle seal.

Treatment

Deep navy or black ground, cyan rim light, gold metal highlights, shallow depth of field and a single hero object. Realistic 3D render, not flat illustration. Article art pairs the render with a bold overlay line.

What to avoid

Gavel-and-handcuffs rhetoric, dollar-bill collages, angry-red fear graphics, and anything that implies a guaranteed legal outcome. The imagery should read as a serious institution, not a late-night advert.

Article art pattern

A single symbolic object at an angle, a blurred institutional background, and one short overlay phrase set in heavy sans. Four examples are hosted in this kit.

Master template rules

Double gold hairline border, inset 18 mm, on every cover.

Logo centred at the top at 22% of cover width. Title in chrome display caps, then a gold serif subtitle, then one hero object in the lower half.

Digital & social applications

YouTube thumbnails, store graphics, the member portal and landing page heroes — all built on the same navy, chrome, cyan and gold system.

YouTube thumbnails — 16:9

Thumbnail rules

Book on the right as a single angled hardcover; headline stacked on the left in gold display caps with a white outline so it survives a 200 px phone view.

No corner logo. The cover already carries the crest, and a second small mark just adds noise at thumb size. Keep headlines to three words per line.

Claims discipline

Headlines describe the subject, not an outcome: “10 Layers of Trust”, not “Protect Everything”. No promise of immunity, no “beat the system”, no fake urgency.

This keeps the channel clear of misleading-claim policies and matches the site's own credibility standard.

Mockups, portal & heroes

Production sizes

YouTube thumbnail 1280×720. Social square 1080×1080. Story and Reel cover 1080×1920. Open Graph image 1200×630. Member portal tile 3:4.

All supplied boards are 1376×768 and crop safely to 16:9.

Portal pattern

One tile per title, cover art on the left or top, gold Download button beneath, title in sans 800. Never repeat a generic tile name across products — each tile must name its own title.

08

Interior & worksheet templates

Editorial pages and fillable forms: a table of contents, chapter openers, checklists, intake forms, the seven-phase playbook and a strategy memo.

Editorial page rules

Light pages for reading: near-white ground, navy header band, gold rules and page numbers. Body set in the reading sans at 10.5–11 pt, one column, generous margins.

Footers carry the crest at small size plus the page number — never the full banner logo.

Form rules

Fields stack in a single column with visible labels above each box, never placeholder-only labels. Checkbox groups sit in two columns with 44 px targets.

All interior copy in these mockups is placeholder — replace it with real doctrine text before publishing.

Seven-phase playbook. Intake → Review → Design → Draft → Sign → Fund → Annual Review. Use these seven short labels; they fit a timeline at any size and stay legible in print.
09

Voice, copywriting & compliance guardrails

Precise, sober, confident. The brand sells clarity and structure, never guarantees or fear. These rules are binding on every headline, caption and cover line.

Tone

Authoritative without bombast. Academic, plain-spoken, unhurried.

Write as a careful instructor: define the term, then show the consequence.

Sentence rhythm

Short declaratives. One idea per sentence. Concrete nouns over abstractions.

Prefer “the trustee holds legal title” to “the trustee is empowered with regard to the holding of title”.

Evidence posture

Cite the principle, the case or the statute. Where a theory fails in court, say so.

Credibility comes from admitting the limits of an argument, not from overstating it.

Person & address

Second person for instruction (“you”), first person plural for the institution (“we teach”).

No slang, no exclamation marks on covers, no emoji in doctrine copy.

Numbers & structure

Number principles in sequence — I through VIII. A numbered spine is part of the brand.

Use en dashes for ranges and the middot for descriptor lockups.

Non-negotiable disclaimer

“For educational purposes only. Not legal advice.”

On every back cover, in every course footer, and beside any downloadable template.

Production checklist

Run this before anything leaves the design file and goes to a printer, a store or a channel.

Logo placed from the real file, not redrawn or regenerated.
Spelling checked at 200 % zoom — titles, subtitles, badges, bullets.
Barcode box left blank, or replaced with the real ISBN/SKU. No invented numbers.
Disclaimer present on every back cover and every template download.
Colours within the documented palette; gold used as an accent only.
Body text at 4.5:1 contrast or better against its ground.
Gold buttons labelled in deep ink, never white.
Thumbnail legible at 200 px wide on a phone.
No immunity, guarantee or urgency claims in any headline.
Print files at 300 DPI with bleed and crop marks; screen files at the stated pixel sizes.
Alt text written for every image published on the web.
Placeholder interior copy replaced with real doctrine text.

Digital export

PNG for graphics with type; JPEG quality 85 for photographic boards. sRGB colour profile. Compress to keep each social asset under about 400 KB.

Print export

PDF/X-4, CMYK, 300 DPI, 3 mm bleed. Convert gold and cyan to spot or process builds tested on the target stock — bright cyan shifts noticeably on uncoated paper.

Known limitations

Stated plainly, because you should hear it here rather than discover it at a printer or in a client review.

  • ! No official brand guideline exists. SPC University publishes no brand book or press kit. This kit was reconstructed from the live site, its artwork and its logo files. Colours, type pairings and rules are an accurate interpretation, not authorised standards.
  • ! The logo is raster only, about 1,600 px wide. There is no vector or transparent-background master. Use the compact banner below 120 px. For print, signage or merchandise, request the original vector art.
  • ! Small on-image lettering degrades. Inside the crest, and on any cover reproduced at tile size, the fine lettering can blur or garble. For print and for hero placements, composite the real logo file over the artwork in your layout tool.
  • ! Four article images are natively 300×169. That is the original size on the site; no larger version exists to fetch. They are fine as thumbnails, not as hero images.
  • ! Interior pages are layout mockups. The copy is placeholder. Replace it with real doctrine text before publishing any volume.
  • ! The two hero options have defects. Option A picked up a badge typo; option B crops several covers. Neither is press-ready — rebuild from the verified front covers.
  • ! Ownership. SPC University's terms of service state that its content, branding, logos and course materials are owned by SPC University. Confirm permission before distributing these assets outside the organisation.
  • ! The lineage of this site. These covers were built for a product line that deliberately excludes certain sovereign-citizen style manuals. Any asset that promises legal immunity is out of scope and intentionally absent here.
