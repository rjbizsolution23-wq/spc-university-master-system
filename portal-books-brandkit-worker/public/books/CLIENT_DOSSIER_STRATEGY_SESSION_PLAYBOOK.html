5. The Compliance Stack (a U.S. person's mandatory disclosures — non-negotiable)

1. Form 3520 — creation of / transfers to a foreign trust; distributions *from* one; foreign gifts/inheritances > $100,000. ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-3520))

2. Form 3520-A — annual foreign-trust owner information (foreign trust with a U.S. owner). ([IRS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i3520a.pdf))

3. FBAR (FinCEN 114) — foreign accounts > $10,000 aggregate.

4. Form 8938 (FATCA) — specified foreign financial assets.

5. Form 709 — gifts above the $19,000 exclusion; GST-exemption allocations for dynasty trusts (miss this and the automatic rules waste exemption).

6. Form 1041 — foreign non-grantor trusts; throwback rules tax accumulated distributions to U.S. beneficiaries.

7. State filings; CRS-equivalent exchange means secrecy is gone — structure for legality, not invisibility. Anyone pitching "undetectable offshore" is selling you willful-BLAST penalties (100% of the account) and prison.

6. Timing and Funding Rules (where "all legal" is won or lost)

▪Fund protective layers before any claim is foreseeable: fraudulent-transfer acts (UVTA) unwind transfers made with intent to hinder present/future creditors; Cook Islands' 2-year window protects the trust, but a U.S. court can still jail a recalcitrant settlor in contempt.
▪Bankruptcy look-back is 10 years for self-settled trusts (11 U.S.C. § 548(e)).
▪Medicaid: 5-year look-back.
▪Retirement assets never go into trusts directly (beneficiary designations instead); S-corp and IRA funding need specialist structuring.
▪Keep trustee/protector powers in hands that are not your creditors' target; you (the settlor) must not be a beneficiary of the APT layer.

1. The System in One Paragraph

Your wealth moves through a layered set of containers. A revocable trust runs daily life and avoids probate. 2. The Money-Flow Story (what actually happens)

1. You gift $19,000/year per person (2026, verified) or larger gifts against your $15M exemption — always with a gift-tax return (709) and Crummey notices.

2. Gifts buy units in the family LLC; appraisals establish their discounted value.

3. Trusts buy or receive those units — Dynasty (GST-exempt), SLATs (spousal access), Cook Islands APT (the shield).

4. Growth compounds inside the trusts, outside your estate.

5. At death, your estate owes no estate tax below $15M/person ($30M married); insurance in the ILIT provides tax-free liquidity; the pour-over will sweeps leftovers into the revocable trust.

6. Generations later, the dynasty trust still holds it all — no estate tax at any generation in between.

5. FAQ

"Is offshore legal?" Yes — with an FSC-licensed Cook Islands trustee, full IRS reporting (3520/3520-A, FBAR, FATCA), and funding before any claims. What's illegal is hiding accounts or deferring known creditors.

"Can I get my money back?" Generally no from the irrevocable layers — that's why the SLAT design (spouse can receive) and L1 revocable spine exist. Design trade-offs, not accidents.

"What if we get sued after funding?" Pre-existing assets are protected; the transfer itself isn't undone if it predated the threat (timing windows: Cook Islands 2 years; U.S. bankruptcy 10 years for self-settled trusts).

"Who pays the taxes on trust income?" Grantor trusts: you do (and that's a feature — extra tax-free gifts). Non-grantor: the trust pays (compressed brackets — distributions to beneficiaries often better).

"What happens when the trustee misbehaves?" Protector removes them; UTC §§ 801–814 duties and surcharge remedies apply.

"Why a Cayman PTC instead of a bank trustee?" Family control + continuity, at a fraction of institutional fees — with professional guardrails (registered office, independent director).

COMMON-LAW EXPRESS TRUSTS (PURE TRUSTS / MASSACHUSETTS TRUSTS) — CONTROLLING SUPREME COURT & HISTORICAL JURISPRUDENCE

1. Foundational Definition and Constitutional Anchor:

An Express Trust under the Common Law (historically termed a Common-Law Pure Trust or Massachusetts Trust) is a private contractual fiduciary relationship created by a Declaration or Indenture of Trust whereby a Settlor/Grantor conveys legal title in property (the Trust Res or Corpus) to one or more Trustees to hold, manage, and administer for the equitable benefit of designated Beneficiaries (or Certificate Holders). Its contractual obligation is protected under Article I, Section 10, Clause 1 of the United States Constitution ('No State shall... pass any... Law impairing the Obligation of Contracts') and traditional Chancery Equity jurisprudence.

2. Alfred D. Chandler — Express Trusts under the Common Law (1912):

Alfred Dupont Chandler's landmark 1912 treatise, 'Express Trusts under the Common Law: A Superior and Distinct Mode of Administration, Distinguished from Partnerships, Contrasted with Corporations', established the classic doctrinal distinction between statutory corporations (which derive their existence from a state legislative franchise) and common-law express trusts (which derive their existence from the natural and common-law right of sui juris individuals to contract and convey property to fiduciaries in equity). Under Chandler's doctrine, when Trustees hold full legal title and plenary management control without partner-style control by beneficiaries, the arrangement is a true trust in equity rather than a partnership or corporate franchise.
